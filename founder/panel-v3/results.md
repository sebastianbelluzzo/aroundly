# Buyer panel: Aroundly, a nightlife app that sells monthly visibility subscriptions to venues

**2 buy · 18 pass** (10% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 7, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- Seeing 2,000+ real monthly active users in my city and a few venues I know who got people through the door from the app, not just views.
- Seeing the app past 2,000 active users in my city and a few venues like mine showing me real people coming through because of it, with the free month guarantee actually honoured.
- Seeing the published monthly active users well past 2,000 in my city, and a stats page showing real views and saves on my nights that I can compare with what my PR guys bring in.
- Seeing real numbers from other venues here: a few thousand active users who are students, and tickets or entries actually coming through the app that I don't already get from my PR.
- Seeing the app live with real monthly users in my city and proof from another venue that it actually brought people through the door on a music night.
- Seeing the published monthly active users pass 2,000 and a couple of venues like mine showing real views and ticket sales in the stats.
- Seeing the monthly active users published and past 2,000, plus a month of free stats showing views and saves on my own nights, with the month-to-month cancel actually honoured.
- Seeing the published monthly active users well past the threshold, and a few venues like mine saying it actually brought people through the door.
- Seeing the app pass 2,000 monthly users in my city, and proof from another venue that it filled their nights, so the fee clearly pays for itself.
- Seeing the app really live with thousands of active users in my city and people showing up at my door saying they found the night on it.
- Seeing the app really past 2,000 active users in my city and a few bars I know showing me extra people through the door because of it, with the first months free.
- Seeing the monthly active users pass 2,000 in my city, and a few clubs nearby telling me it actually brought people on a Saturday.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
