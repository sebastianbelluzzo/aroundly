Aroundly, a new app (launching soon, not public yet) for nightlife in Friuli and Veneto: people open it and see what is on tonight near them, on a map and a calendar, with the full flyer (time, DJ, genre, price), and can save, share or buy entry.
For venues, three monthly packages (launch price, list price in brackets, + VAT): Venue €50 (€80) for bars: official profile linked to your socials, unlimited events, a spot on the map, guided setup.
Stage €150 (€220) for venues with DJ sets: closer map spot, 2 "Recommended" badges a month, push notifications to followers and 1 area push a month, basic stats dashboard, ticket sales.
Headline €250 (€400) for clubs: 4 events at the top of the home a month, 4 "Trending" badges, 4 area pushes, video trailer, advanced dashboard with monthly PDF report.
First month free with no card, then a 3-month paid minimum, monthly renewal, cancel with 30 days' notice. The founders set it up for you; loading a night takes about 2 minutes.
