# Buyer panel: Aroundly, a nightlife app that sells monthly visibility subscriptions to venues

**1 buy · 19 pass** (5% buy) out of 20 simulated buyers. Seed 7, so the same cards can be dealt again.

These are simulated buyers, not customers. Use this to find objections and weak spots, then confirm the big ones with real people before you spend.

## What would flip a no

- Seeing the app already past 2,000 active users in my city, with other clubs I know on it and proof that people on it actually showed up at their doors.
- Seeing a published user count above 2,000 in my city and a few venues I know saying it actually sent people through the door.
- Published proof of thousands of real monthly users in my city, many of them students, and ticket sales or a feature that brings people my PR and lists don't already reach.
- Seeing the app pass the 2,000 monthly users in my city and a few nearby venues showing real numbers of people who came because of it, with the 500-views guarantee actually honoured.
- Seeing a real, published number of local users and a couple of bars near me who got people through the door from it.
- Published numbers showing well over 2,000 monthly active users in my city, and proof that other clubs near me got real people through the door from it.
- Published monthly users above 2,000 in my city, plus proof from another bar that the stats on views and saves match people actually showing up on music nights.
- Seeing a published user count well past 2,000 in my city and proof that people from the app actually walk in on my DJ nights.
- Proof from another bar in my area that the app filled their nights, plus a result tied to real people at the door, not just views.
- Seeing real numbers, past the 2,000 users, and proof from another venue near me that the app brought people through the door who didn't come from Instagram.
- Proof, from a venue like mine, that the paid push brought in more paying customers than it cost, with no work from me.
- A published monthly active user count well past 2,000 in my city, and a nearby club telling me the app actually brought people through the door.

Buyers say they would buy **1.0 times** in the first month on average.
